# Dazzling Diva Design Reference

## Source Assets

- `Main Container.svg`
- `Main Container.png`
- `Main Container.pdf`

## Reference Dimensions

- Desktop artboard width: `1440px`
- Desktop artboard height: approximately `7507px`

## Visual Direction

- Black foundation with deep maroon promotional surfaces
- High-contrast white editorial typography
- Large fashion-led hero and editorial mosaic sections
- Compact product cards with quick actions
- Deep maroon footer with commerce utility links

## Section Mapping

- Hero banner with dual CTA emphasis
- Category showcase cards
- Flash deals countdown banner
- Featured product strip
- Editorial split promotions
- New arrivals grid
- Large editorial mosaic with collection CTAs
- Product spotlight trio
- Festive campaign CTA banner
- Service-benefit strip
- Footer with policy, shopping, account, and brand columns

## Responsive Interpretation

- Preserve editorial hierarchy on desktop and laptop widths.
- Reflow category, product, and mosaic grids into stacked layouts on tablet and mobile.
- Keep promotional panels image-led, but suppress incomplete sections instead of rendering broken placeholders.

## Assumptions

- The provided fashion photography is reference-only and will not be shipped as production catalog content.
- Typography will use open-source close matches through `next/font`.
- Empty catalog states should keep the luxury tone through layout, spacing, and branded messaging.
